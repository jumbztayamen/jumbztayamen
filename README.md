## What I work on

Most of my background is in:
- Infrastructure and platform architecture  
- IT service management and operations (incident, change, DR/BCP)  
- Shared services and vendor governance  
- Systems that have regulatory, audit, or availability pressure  

Recently, I’ve been spending more time on **AI/ML**, particularly where it intersects with:
- Operations and reliability  
- Decision support (not black-box automation)  
- Risk, anomaly detection, and forecasting  
- Turning operational data into something leadership can act on  

I care less about models in isolation and more about **where they fit in real systems**.

## How I think about AI

AI is only useful if:
- the data is clean and traceable  
- the outputs can be explained  
- the system can fail safely  
- and the business knows when *not* to trust it  

Most of my AI work focuses on **augmenting human decisions**, not replacing them.
